# DS2 go-to-market plan — SMBs in Greece, Cyprus & London

*Synthesized 2026-07-16 from a 4-strategist + 2-hostile-critic workflow run. This is
the pruned plan of record — the version that survived a skeptical Thessaloniki
business owner and a feasibility audit capped at 15 founder-hours/week. The full raw
strategy material lives in the workflow transcript.*

---

## The thesis

**The company is the demo.** DS2 never argues "we can build any digital solution" —
that's every agency's empty claim. Instead, every funnel stage hands the prospect a
working artifact built on the same stack we'd build for them:

> We run our company on the software we sell you. Our leads, our calendar, our notes,
> our client analytics and an AI copilot that remembers every decision live in an
> admin panel we built for ourselves. The assistant that answers our line in Greek
> and English is a product on this list. The audit of your website was run by our own
> tool before we ever met you.

Everything below serves that thesis.

## Two beachheads only (the critics' hardest ruling)

Six segments across three geographies = zero compounding for two founders. Launch
with exactly two, each anchored to proof that already exists:

**1. Athens SMBs in niches with shipped proof** — gyms/fitness (the shipped gym
site is the door-opener) + the second client's category. Phone-first outbound powered
by the lead-finder. Expand niche-by-niche only after the playbook converts.

**2. London Greek/Cypriot diaspora businesses** — Palmers Green, Southgate, the
Finchley corridor: law firms, estate agents, food importers, clinics. They buy at
London rates but trust in Greek; the Athens–London identity IS the pitch. Warm,
relationship-led, no cold email needed.

**Deferred, with triggers:** Cyprus (relationship market, cold channels legally out —
enter via accountant/partner referrals once the Greek playbook converts; grant-wave
language stays in the drawer until a call actually opens). Multi-location/Panoptes
(event-driven — keep the report as an upsell, don't hunt it). London MTD practices
(the 7 Aug 2026 deadline is unreachable; revisit ahead of the April 2027 £30k
threshold). E-invoicing SMEs — never direct; see the accountant channel.

## The offer architecture — 3 client-facing, 8 internal

Client-facing (what the site and the pitch say):

| Offer | What it is | Price posture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The site check-up** | Fixed-fee audit of their site (speed, accessibility, SEO, trust), walked through in person. The wedge. | GR €290–490 / London £450–900, **credited in full against any build** |
| **The build** | Whatever the diagnosis says: a website that earns trust, one panel that runs the business, or a desktop app your team installs. Scoped after a paid discovery. | GR €3.5k–18k / London £9k–45k by scope |
| **The monthly** | Stewardship + the recurring services: front desk that never sleeps (Xenia), get paid without chasing (Plutus), your market watched weekly (Argus). | GR €150–400/mo per service / London £300–800/mo |

The 8 named products stay as the **internal menu** — the demo is matched to the
prospect's pain (hospitality/clinic → Xenia; invoice-heavy → Plutus; competitive
retail → Argus). Framed as *"tools we use inside engagements"*, never as a 6-product
line from a 2-person firm.

**Pricing rules that survived the critics:** fixed and capped first fees, always
(the "burned by an agency" counter). Flat pilot fees, never per-outcome pricing
(invites disputes). The check-up fee always credits against the build.

## Copy (pruned)

Keep:
- *We run our company on the software we sell you.*
- *A front desk that answers at eleven at night, in Greek and in English.*
- *One screen that shows you your whole business.*
- *A desktop app your team installs — your company's information, the same on every machine.*
- *We'd rather challenge your idea in the first meeting than bill for it in the sixth.*
- *"This creates risk because…" — the four words you'll hear from us most.*
- *You don't need to decide now. Take your time — we're happy to proceed either way.*

**Killed by the critics — do not use:**
- ~~"I ran an audit on your site and found problems"~~ as a cold opener (the exact
  spam template every London owner deletes).
- ~~"The homework we brought was done by an agent we built"~~ (confesses the
  personalization is automated — instant bin).
- ~~Any claim that ΕΣΠΑ vouchers are currently available~~ (cycles are CLOSED as of
  mid-2026 — verified). Only permitted line: *"we spec it grant-ready so you can
  apply the day the next call opens."*

This is also the only door into e-invoicing money — honest-broker, never posing as a myDATA provider. |
| 4 | **London diaspora, warm** | 2h | Community relationships in Greek — coffees, introductions through the two clients' networks and the Hellenic business community. The admin-panel walkthrough replaces the case-study portfolio they'll ask for. |
| 5 | **LinkedIn as air cover** | 1h | ONE post/week alternating founders (data teardown / build-in-public), plus replying to prospects who check the profiles after calls. Fixed headlines: plain-language category + Athens & London. Not a content program. |

Cut for now: events/chambers (easiest way to feel busy without selling), the
nurture newsletter, UK cold email (revisit month 3 with LIA documentation and a
warmed domain — and never with the audit-opener).

## The proof kit — build order tied to pipeline, not hope

**Now (this fortnight, ~3 founder-days):**
1. **Demo-mode toggle on the admin panel** (seeded fake client data — also the GDPR
   answer for screen-sharing) — the flagship walkthrough asset.
2. **Case one-pagers** for both shipped sites with live analytics charts — "projects
   end; responsibility doesn't", proven.
3. **Aegis in concierge mode** — a founder runs it manually per booked prospect
   (minutes each). The self-serve landing page waits until call volume justifies it.
4. Copilot memory set-piece: 3 rehearsed prompts that reliably land. A set piece in
   the demo, not the headline.

**Triggered (build when the pipeline says so):**
- **Xenia public demo line** ("call this number, book an appointment, in Greek") —
  2–3 days, build when the first clinic/hospitality meetings are booked. The single
  strongest GR demo asset.
- **Desktop-app demo installer** — build at the first desktop-app prospect;
  meanwhile the film's mockup #5 carries the claim.
- **Industry mockup kit** (fitness / hospitality / clinics) — falls out of the film's
  mockups 3–6 nearly for free.
- Plutus/Fama sandboxes — phase in behind real demand.

**The film** (*The honest version*, [docs/video/FILM-DIRECTION.md](../video/FILM-DIRECTION.md)):
the Brand Upgrade proof — it IS the class of artifact a Brand Upgrade client buys.
Its six mockups double as the industry mockup kit. Website hero + LinkedIn + the
first 90 seconds of every pitch. Don't block launch on it.

**The rebuild showpiece** ([REBUILDS-SHOWPIECE.md](REBUILDS-SHOWPIECE.md)): the
fictional tragic site ("Achilleon Gym", Kallithea) rebuilt to premium in a 3-week
gated timeline — the proof asset that sells "the build" and visualizes what the
check-up finds. Master asset `/rebuilds` (3–4 days), then presenter mode, one-pager,
social cut, and industry variants that let outreach link a fictional mirror instead
of critiquing the prospect's own site.

## The 15-minute discovery demo (the conversion moment)

Pre-call, day before (15 min): lead-finder brief + Aegis run on their site + pick ONE
product matched to their pain + switch admin to demo mode + seed one copilot memory
about their industry.

1. **Open with THEIR audit** (5 min) — "before we talk about us: here's your site."
   Three findings, one named risk, protective framing.
2. **The walkthrough** (7 min) — "this runs our company": funnel → two-way calendar →
   copilot recalls the seeded memory → analytics. Then the one matched product, live.
3. **The pause** (3 min) — "You don't need to decide now." Leave the check-up offer
   and the one-pager. Follow up on Viber/WhatsApp, their choice.

Crash fallback: the 90-second screen film, downloaded locally.

## Objection counters (the ones that matter)

- **"We got burned by an agency before."** → Fixed price, capped, credited. First
  engagement small by design. *"We'd rather challenge your idea in the first meeting
  than bill for it in the sixth."*
- **"My nephew handles the website."** → Never attack the nephew. *"Keep him — we'll
  give him better foundations to maintain."*
- **"Why not just use [SaaS tool]?"** → *"Sometimes that IS the right answer — and
  if it is, the diagnosis will say so. You'd be paying us to tell you the truth, not
  to sell you a build."* (This answer converts more diagnostics than it loses builds.)
- **"Is there a subsidy?"** → The honest version only: grant-ready spec, apply when
  a call opens.

## Calendar reality (it's mid-July)

- **Now–July 31:** the last Greek selling window before August. Demo mode + case
  one-pagers built THIS week; call blocks run immediately; warm referral asks to both
  clients in person.
- **August (Greece is dead):** shift to London diaspora coffees; build the September
  assets (film, Xenia demo line, mockup kit); accountant conversations (they're
  prepping clients for 1 Oct).
- **September:** full Greek sprint — pipeline booked in July, accountant channel
  warm, film live on the site.
- **Expectations (critic-calibrated):** first signed deal in 30–60 days via the
  referral loop; first outbound-sourced win day 60–90; burned owners take 6–10 weeks
  and refer hardest afterwards. At 6h/week of calls: 25–30 dials → 6–8 conversations
  → 1–2 diagnosis meetings weekly.

## Compliance spine (non-negotiable, half a day once)

Greek Article 11 opt-out registry scrub before every call list, 30-day refresh. No
cold calls to numbers on the registries. Cyprus: no cold outreach at all (opt-in
extends to legal persons) — warm intros only. UK (when it opens): corporate
subscribers only, LIA documented, no audit-opener.

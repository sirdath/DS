# The rebuild showpiece — "Achilleon Gym & Fitness"

*The tragic-site → premium-rebuild proof asset. Designed 2026-07-16 (3-agent
workflow: before-site architect / format strategist / copywriter). Sells "the build"
offer in the GTM plan; complies with POSITIONING §10 (Transformation Story format,
never a naked before/after slider; never shaming).*

## The fiction

**Achilleon Gym & Fitness** — family-run neighbourhood gym in Kallithea, Athens.
Opened 2003 by Achilleas Vamvakas, a former wrestling coach, now run with his son
Petros. The site was built in 2017 by a longtime member who "knew computers," and
nobody has had the heart or the time to touch it since November 2020.

**Why a family gym:** it hits both beachheads at once. An Athens gym owner is looking
at their own site (the shipped gym case study is the follow-through). A London
Greek-Cypriot professional is looking at "the gym my uncle goes to in Palmers Green" —
and every flaw transfers one-to-one to their vertical: timetable→fee schedule,
classes→consultations, Viber→WhatsApp. One showpiece, four verticals. And crucially:
everyone loves their neighbourhood gym, so the Before earns **affection, not
contempt** — the audience roots for Achilleas. That's the protective register the
brand law demands.

## The tragedy catalog (14 flaws, all real-world common)

Every flaw is one the lead-finder fingerprints on real sites; each maps to a fix
phrased as a decision (brand law: decisions, not features):

| The flaw | The decision it becomes |
| --- | --- |
| Auto-rotating hero slider, stretched stock model, 'WELCOME TO OUR GYM' drop-shadow | Chose one message for the first five seconds — a real photo, one sentence, one action |
| Desktop-only rendering on phones (pinch-zoom postage stamp) | Rebuilt for the phone first, because that's where members actually find you |
| The timetable is a **JPEG photo of the printed A4 on the front-desk wall** (winter 2023 programme) | Made the timetable a page, not a photo — updated from your phone in under a minute |
| No prices — "Επικοινωνήστε μαζί μας για τιμές" | Put prices on the page, so the calls that come are from people ready to join |
| News page: last post "Καλή Χρονιά 2019!", footer "© 2018" | Removed the news page nobody had time to feed — freshness now comes from reviews, not resolutions |
| **Fossilised COVID banner: "…λειτουργούμε με ραντεβού — Νοέμβριος 2020"** (it's 2026) | Gave every announcement an expiry date, so the site can never say 2020 in 2026 |
| Contact map is a grey "For development purposes only" rectangle | Replaced the broken map with a pin that opens navigation — and a photo of the actual door |
| Contact form silently dead since a 2021 hosting migration | Made every contact route end at your phone — call, Viber, or WhatsApp, one tap |
| American stock-model photography in a big-box gym that looks nothing like Kallithea | Shot the real gym — the place and the people are the product, not the stock library |
| Template leftovers in English through the Greek content ("READ MORE", "Posted by admin") | Gave the site one voice — every word chosen, nothing left over from the template |
| Plain HTTP — "Not secure" beside the address on the contact page | Made the browser vouch for the site instead of warning about it |
| Tab reads "Αρχική – Just another WordPress site", grey default favicon | Named every page for how customers search, not how the theme was installed |
| Nine-item menu (Home · About · Our Philosophy · Services · Gallery · News · Downloads…) | Restructured the menu around the three questions every member asks — when, how much, where |
| Phone number rendered as an image at the page bottom | (folds into the contact-route decision) |

**Realism discipline:** era-lock everything to one 2017 build moment
(Avada/BeTheme-style theme, Revolution Slider, Roboto Condensed, orange-on-charcoal
`#ff6600`/`#222`, parallax stock dividers). Residual competence everywhere — someone
cared once. Tragic, never parody.

**The After:** the premium DS2 aesthetic — real warm photography (evening light on
the gym floor, a coach taping wrists), frosted glass, editorial serif, mobile-first —
per the coveted-designs direction in [FILM-DIRECTION.md](../video/FILM-DIRECTION.md).

## Formats, in build order

1. **`/rebuilds` on ds2-consulting.com** (3–4 days — the master asset) — standalone
   route linked from Work, deliberately NOT touching the homepage (which is under the
   do-not-deploy-rebuild rule). Full Transformation Story: snapshot → challenge →
   decisions → same-viewport pairs → timeline → outcome → CTA.
2. **Presenter mode** (`/rebuilds?present=1`, ~1 day) — the discovery-call beat:
   founder steps through the pairs one caption at a time.
3. **Printable one-pager** (A4, Greek + English, Viber-forwardable tall image, ~1 day)
   — survives "let me think about it."
4. **30–45s social cut** (9:16 + 1:1, GR/EN, 1–2 days) — Instagram for Athens gyms,
   LinkedIn/Facebook for the diaspora.
5. **Industry variants** (~1 day each, later) — clinic/law/lettings versions so
   lead-finder outreach can link a *fictional* mirror instead of critiquing the
   prospect's own site (never attack the nephew).
6. **Film handoff** (0.5 day) — the same screens feed the brand film's rebuild beat.
   One fictional business, seen everywhere.

## The five same-viewport pairs (brand law: same crop, before faded left)

1. **Mobile hero** 390×844 — the pinch-zoom postage stamp vs. the thumb-first hero.
2. **Desktop hero** 1440×900 — slider mid-transition vs. one photo, one sentence, one button.
3. **The money crop** — the JPEG timetable photo vs. the tap-friendly schedule with prices.
4. **Trust crop** — Gmail + dead icons + "© 2019" vs. map pin, tap-to-call, live reviews.
5. **Story crop** — justified text wall + pixelated stock vs. editorial about-section with real photography.

## Copy (approved pool)

**Section:** label **Rebuilds** · headline recommendation: **"What three weeks can
change"** (alternates: "Same business, new first impression" / "The site your work
deserves").

**The argument** (the founder's thesis in DS2 voice — centerpiece of the section):

> Your website is where strangers decide what your business is worth — before they
> call, before they walk in, before you get a word in. You've built something real,
> and when the site doesn't show it, every visitor quietly prices you below what
> you're actually worth. That's not embarrassing; it's expensive — because people
> don't invest in a business that looks like it hasn't been invested in. We build
> sites that carry the weight of what you've actually built.

**Pricing frame** (candid premium):

> We'll be straight about the price, because we'd rather lose you now than surprise
> you later: this is the premium option. A build like this runs €3.5k–8k in Greece
> and £9k–18k in London, and before we start you'll know exactly which number and
> why. For that, your site goes live in under three weeks — structure, copy,
> photography direction, launch. The three-week promise covers exactly that scope:
> the brand-site rebuild. It doesn't cover booking engines or admin panels — if you
> need those, we scope them honestly as their own piece.

**CTA:** *Want a similar result? Start with the site check-up — €290–490 fixed,
credited in full if we build. Prefer the phone? Call or Viber us — we answer, and
you'll be speaking with a founder.*
